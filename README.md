# VaiBReport — Nine platforms monitored daily. Claude writes the editorial. No filler.

VaiBReport is a curated intelligence feed for builders and founders. Nine platforms — GitHub, HuggingFace, Replicate, Papers with Code, npm/PyPI, GitLab, Ollama, and the DevHunt/HN/BetaList/Uneed cluster — are monitored daily by automated pipelines. Claude applies quality filters and writes the editorial. When there's signal, a digest publishes. When there isn't, nothing runs.

## The Problem

You're spending 30–45 minutes every morning across GitHub Trending, HuggingFace, Hacker News, and four newsletters trying to find the two or three things that actually matter. Most days you find nothing. Some days you miss something you should have caught. The feeds you're reading are either too noisy (Product Hunt: consumer audience optimization), too narrow (GitHub Trending: one platform, virality-skewed), or too committed to a publication schedule (newsletters: runs daily whether or not there's anything worth saying). None of them have your standards. None of them cover all nine platforms.

## What This Does

- **9-platform coverage** — GitHub, HuggingFace, Replicate, Papers with Code, npm/PyPI, GitLab, Ollama, DevHunt/HN/BetaList/Uneed. One feed, consistent filter.
- **Quality threshold over frequency** — if the signal is weak that day, no post runs. Silence is better than filler. This is enforced algorithmically, not by human discipline.
- **Claude editorial** — not algorithm-ranked. Not human-curated with publication deadlines. Claude applies structured quality filters and writes the breakdown.
- **Builder/founder filter** — every item evaluated for practitioner utility. Star thresholds. Cooldown dedup (no re-featuring the same repo in close succession). Adoption momentum over launch-day noise.
- **Local AI coverage** — dedicated Ollama section. Models you can run locally, no API key required, covered before they hit general tech press.
- **Full searchable archive** — every item ever featured, permanent, filterable. The catalog compounds in value over time.
- **Free. No login.** Zero friction.

## Who It's For

**The Tool-Stacker** — solo builder shipping multiple SaaS projects per year. Has a Notion page full of tools to evaluate. Finds Product Hunt too noisy, HN too scattered. Wants someone to filter the firehose with their standards, not the platform algorithm's.

**The Ecosystem Watcher** — technical founder who's been surprised twice by capabilities they didn't know competitors were using. Needs signal on gaining traction, not viral moments.

**The Chaos Desktop User** — developer interested in local AI and open-source tooling. VaiBReport surfaces new Ollama models and practical HuggingFace Spaces before they reach general tech press.

## Why Not Just Read Hacker News?

Hacker News is community-ranked. Popular posts reflect the community's full range of interests — politics, philosophy, culture, technical debates — not your tooling stack. VaiBReport applies one filter: builder utility, gaining adoption, practical implementation. GitHub Trending is virality-skewed — the most-starred repos of the day are often a function of social media momentum, not real adoption curves. And newsletters have publication frequency commitments. They run whether or not there's anything real that day. VaiBReport only runs when there is. That structural difference is why readers trust it.
