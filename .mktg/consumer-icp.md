## The Page

---

### You can't keep up. Nobody can.

A hundred new AI tools ship every week. GitHub, HuggingFace, Product Hunt, papers with code — the signal is everywhere and the noise is deafening.

You're running a company, building a product, or advising clients. You don't have time to monitor 9 platforms, filter out the academic noise, and figure out what's actually useful for someone in your situation.

That's what VaiBReport does.

---

### What it is

VaiBReport is a daily digest of what actually matters in the AI tools space — curated for founders, solopreneurs, and the people who advise them.

Every day, it monitors GitHub, HuggingFace, Replicate, GitLab, npm, PyPI, Ollama, Papers with Code, and Product Hunt. It filters by quality signals (stars, downloads, recency, momentum). It drops the junk. Then it writes you a plain-English briefing — no hype words, no filler, just what it does and why a builder would care.

**It's the thing you wish existed when you were trying to figure out if there's a better way to do what you're currently paying $400/month for.**

---

### Who it's for

**If you're a founder with a small team:**
You need to know what tools exist before you hire for a role. The right tool at the right time means you don't need that hire yet. VaiBReport surfaces those tools before your competitors find them.

**If you're a solopreneur:**
You're one person running everything. The right tool doesn't just save time — it changes what's possible for you to do alone. VaiBReport filters the catalog for tools a solo operator can actually run.

**If you're pre-MVP:**
Stack decisions made in week one are lived with for years. VaiBReport is how you find out what experienced builders are actually using right now — not what a blog post from 2023 recommended.

**If you're advising businesses on tools:**
Your clients trust you to know what's out there. VaiBReport is background intelligence — the thing you check so you can say "I looked at everything in this category and here's why I'm recommending this."

---

### What you get

**The daily digest**
One post per day. Every category covered — AI/LLM tools, dev infrastructure, UGC and content tools, no-code, data, deployment, and more. Each item gets a one-liner that tells you exactly what it does and who it's for. Not twenty words of SEO. Twenty words of signal.

**The catalog**
Every tool that's ever surfaced in the digest, browsable and searchable. Filter by category, source, ICP, or hotness. Sort by recency, stars, downloads, or streak. Find that thing you half-remember seeing three weeks ago.

**The leaderboard**
Which tools have been surfacing repeatedly. Which ones are rising fast. Which ones have been featured the most. A live pulse on what's getting traction in the builder community.

**Research reports (on request)**
Submit a topic — "I need to understand the AI video generation space" or "what are my options for self-hosted auth?" — and get back a structured research report: landscape overview, tool comparison table, ICP-specific recommendations, and an honest pick. The kind of report you used to pay a consultant to produce.

---

### What makes it different

Most tool aggregators are lists. VaiBReport is **opinionated**.

It doesn't just surface tools — it filters them, contextualises them for specific audiences, and tells you when something is wrong for your situation. The consulting notes layer, the ICP use cases, the "honest take" — these are the things that turn a list of URLs into actual intelligence.

And it's updated every single day. Not weekly. Not "when we get to it." Every morning, the pipeline runs and the catalog grows.

---

### The honest version

It's not perfect. The AI-generated blurbs are occasionally wrong. Some tools don't have great landing pages and the screenshots show it. A few sources surface academic work that's interesting but not production-ready.

But it's the most thorough automated sweep of the AI tools landscape available for free, updated daily, with no VC-funded startup telling you what to think about it.
